Welcome to URL & Message Scanner

This extension is offline by default. It scans links and messages locally using rules and heuristics.

What is sent online?

  • Only when you explicitly enable online threat checks in settings.
  • Only the URL is sent for lookup — not your full chat history or password text.
  • Only to the Google Safe Browsing API and VirusTotal API, under the host permissions configured in the extension.
  • Never to analytics, telemetry, or remote config servers.

What is stored?

  • API keys are stored in chrome.storage.local only.
  • They are never synced to Chrome sync, logged, or sent to a telemetry endpoint.
  • The extension may cache results for one hour to reduce repeat lookups.

Important

Results are heuristic and informational only. They are not a guarantee of safety and they do not guarantee a site is malicious or safe.
